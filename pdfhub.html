Trade-off, honestly stated

Because pages become images, text stops being selectable/searchable in the output. This works well for scans and image-heavy PDFs; it's the wrong tool for a text-only report you still need to search.

How it works

The whole point of the architecture: the PDF engine runs where your files already are.

01

Choose a file

Drag it in, or pick it from your device. It loads into this tab's memory only.

02

Set the options

Pick pages, order, rotation or quality — whatever the tool needs from you.

03

Process locally

An in-browser PDF engine does the actual work. Nothing is sent anywhere.

04

Download the result

The output is generated as a file on your device and offered straight to you.

The privacy claim, precisely

Worth being exact about, instead of just promising "we delete your files."

What actually happens

Every tool on this page runs entirely with in-browser JavaScript libraries. Your file is read into this tab's memory, transformed there, and offered back to you as a download — it never leaves your device over the network, because there is no upload step to begin with.

What this build doesn't include (yet)

Accounts, history, subscriptions, OCR, Office conversions and e-signatures are on the roadmap in the plan but intentionally not built here — the MVP philosophy is six tools working well before anything else.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a file size limit?

Only the practical limit of your device's memory and browser — very large files (500MB+ scans) may be slow since everything runs on your machine's CPU.

Do you keep a copy of my file?

No. There's no upload endpoint in this build, so there's nothing on a server to keep.

Will this work on my phone?

Yes — drag-and-drop falls back to a normal file picker on mobile, and every tool is usable on a small screen.

Can I unlock a password-protected PDF here?

Not in this build. Encrypted PDFs will show a clear error rather than fail silently or half-process.
